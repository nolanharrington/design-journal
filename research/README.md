# Research and evidence

Use [the research summary template](../templates/research-summary.md) for
`YYYY-MM-DD-short-topic.md`. Capture the question, method, findings, limitations,
and implications, using only information approved for public release.

Keep raw recordings, transcripts, personal details, and confidential artifacts
outside this repository. Avoid small-group details that could identify people.
Use generic restricted-source references where necessary; do not embed internal
URLs or imply the source has been independently verified by the agent.

Link summaries to the decisions they inform. Include counter-evidence and
distinguish what was observed from what the designer inferred.
