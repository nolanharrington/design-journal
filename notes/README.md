# Working notes

Capture enough to remember what happened and why it mattered. Rough bullets
are welcome; you do not need a polished decision record for every activity.

Copy [the note template](../templates/note.md) to
`YYYY-MM-DD-short-topic.md`. Record the event date separately if writing later.
Link approved research, decisions, and impact records as they emerge.

Keep observations distinct from your interpretation. Preserve original notes
when asking an agent for a synthesis. Do not include raw interviews, participant
identifiers, private links, or confidential meeting content.

See [the fictional example](example-fictional.md) for a short entry.
