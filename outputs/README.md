# Case studies and performance reviews

Copy [the case-study template](../templates/case-study.md) or
[the performance-review template](../templates/performance-review.md) here with
a descriptive name. Keep outputs as drafts until the designer reviews accuracy,
privacy, contribution, and permission to publish.

Build the narrative from project context, notes, research, decisions, and impact
records. Link each substantive claim to supporting evidence. Do not use the
fictional example as evidence. Mark unsupported claims for clarification rather
than turning them into accomplishments.

Explain the designer's individual contribution separately from team delivery.
Keep limitations, unmeasured outcomes, and learning visible. Record substantial
AI assistance without implying the agent has approved publication.
